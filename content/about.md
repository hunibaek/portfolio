Seunghoon Baek (b. 1990, South Korea) is a visual artist living and working in Berlin.

His work moves between sculpture, performance, photography, sound and video installation, and deals with light, shadow, perception and digital light as a metaphor for consciousness and simulation. Concepts from Buddhist philosophy — emptiness, form and emptiness, dependent origination — run through his practice.

Recent works use UV light and photochromic pigment: matter darkens under UV while shadows turn bright.

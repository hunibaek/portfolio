

# Bio

Seunghoon Baek (b. 1990, South Korea) is a media-performance artist based in Berlin & South Korea.

He creates performances and installations through artificial light and darkness. Light makes everything visible, yet remains itself unseen. He takes light, which has always been a medium for seeing something else, and turns it back into an object of observation.

The artist regards the world we see as a virtual construct woven by perception. Light that reaches the eye becomes information, and that information is woven into a single world. Light, then, is the very site where this virtuality takes shape.

In his work, light is passed on, covered, and leaves traces on matter. Where light falls, surfaces darken, while shadows grow bright. The moment light becomes something one can touch, the act of seeing itself comes into view. To materialize light is to materialize perception.

He understands light as a compression of information. Today the world reaches us as light, through screens and optical fibres, and we live gazing at that light almost without pause. In an age when everything arrives compressed into a single flash, how can perception reveal itself as matter? Where light and darkness presuppose each other, the one who sees and what is seen fold into one.

# 소개

백승훈은 베를린예술대학교(UdK)에서 모니카 본비치니(Monica Bonvicini)를 사사하며 석사를, 할레 부르크 기비헨슈타인 예술대학에서 디플롬을 마쳤다. 2024년에는 UdK 베를린에서 장소특정적 세미나 「Place and Perception」을 이끌었다. 2022년 독일 연방 미술대학생상(Bundespreis für Kunststudierende) 후보에 올랐으며, 유럽미디어아트페스티벌(EMAF)을 비롯해 독일·한국·아이슬란드·쿠바에서 작품을 선보였다.

# Über

Seunghoon Baek studierte Bildende Kunst bei Monica Bonvicini an der Universität der Künste Berlin und schloss zuvor sein Diplom an der Burg Giebichenstein Kunsthochschule Halle ab. 2024 leitete er an der UdK Berlin das ortsspezifische Seminar *Place and Perception*. 2022 wurde er für den Bundespreis für Kunststudierende nominiert. Seine Arbeiten wurden unter anderem beim European Media Art Festival (EMAF) sowie in Deutschland, Südkorea, Island und Kuba gezeigt.

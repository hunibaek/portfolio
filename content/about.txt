# Bio

Seunghoon Baek (b. 1990, South Korea) is a media-performance artist based in Berlin.

# Education

2022–2024 Master Universität der Künste Berlin, Bildende Kunst (Prof. Monica Bonvicini)
2016-2022 Diplom Burg Giebichenstein Halle, Time based art (Prof. Michaela Schweiger)
2019–2020 Guest studies, Academy of Fine Arts Munich (Prof. Olaf Nicolai)
2009–2014 Bachelor Painting and Photography, Keimyung University, Daegu, South Korea

# Teaching

2024 *Place and Perception – A Site-Specific Seminar*, Seminar Supervisor, Universität der Künste Berlin

# Solo Exhibition

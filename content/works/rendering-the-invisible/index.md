---
title: Rendering the Invisible
year: 2026
medium: Installation, performance — stainless steel, UV LED, photochromic paint, aluminium profile
order: 1
---
UV light darkens matter while shadows turn bright — a paradoxical reversal of how we expect light and shadow to behave. The work treats digital light as a metaphor for consciousness and simulation.

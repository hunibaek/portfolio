title: The Person Who Cannot See Himself
year: 2026
medium: Interactive installation, 2 laser levels, glow paint, 2 construction tripods
video: https://youtu.be/XoTxn9bRYoY
---
Düsseldorf, Germany

When a viewer casts a shadow over the laser beam, phosphorescent text gradually appears beneath the shadow. The text consists of excerpts from the artist’s short story, which follows a figure wandering across an endless horizon in search of an invisible body.

“As the sun sets, my shadow fades. Afraid of disappearing, I run toward the sun.”

“On the water, there is another sun. I try to hold the ripples, but it grows more distorted.”

“Transferring consciousness into darkness.”
